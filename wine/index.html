Fortification — Alcohol Levels

Fino SherryFortified to ~15% → flor develops
Oloroso SherryFortified to ~17%+ → no flor, oxidative ageing
PortFortified during fermentation → ~19-22% final
Key distinction — Port vs Sherry fortification:
Port: spirit added during fermentation → stops yeast → retains grape sugar → wine is sweet
Sherry: fermented to dry first, then spirit added after → level of fortification determines Fino (15%) vs Oloroso (17%+)

Common confusions:
Gewurztraminer = floral (rose) + LOW acidity. Not herbaceous (that's Sauvignon Blanc)
Macon = simple, fruity, unoaked Chardonnay. Not barrel-fermented (that's Meursault, Côte d'Or)
Sauvignon Blanc = cool + moderate. Not hot climate
Hot climate = high alcohol + high tannin + deeper colour + full body — all of the above
Pinot Grigio vs Pinot Gris: same grape. PG (Italy) = light, dry, neutral. Gris (Alsace) = richer, fuller

Sherry
Spain — fortified after fermentation. Ranges from bone-dry to very sweet. Aged using the Solera system.

Key concept — Flor vs Oxidative:
Flor (Fino): layer of yeast protects wine from oxygen → stays pale, light, fresh
Oxidative (Oloroso): no flor → exposed to oxygen → becomes darker, richer, nuttier
Amontillado: starts under flor, then flor dies → gets both characters
Port
Portugal — fortified during fermentation to retain sweetness. All styles are sweet.
StyleColour IntensityColourAgeingKey Characteristics
Ruby Deep Ruby / Purple Large vessels, short ageing Fruity, simple, youthful
Reserve Ruby Deep Ruby Large vessels, longer than Ruby More complex and concentrated than Ruby
Late Bottled Vintage (LBV) Deep Ruby / Garnet Large vessels, 4–6 years More complex, often ready to drink
Vintage Deep Ruby → Garnet (with age) Bottled young, ages in bottle for decades Most premium; tannic when young, needs long bottle ageing
Tawny Medium Tawny / Brown Small barrels, oxidative, extended Nutty, dried fruit, caramel, deliberate oxidation
Key concept — Colour & Ageing:
Ruby styles → aged in large vessels (minimal oxygen) → retains deep colour and fruit
Tawny → aged in small barrels (more oxygen) → loses intensity → medium colour, tawny-brown hue, nutty/dried fruit
